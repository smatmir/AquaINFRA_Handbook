# Why the Vantaanjoki

<p class="chapter-meta">Chapter 1 of 4</p>

<div class="chapter-intro">
    <p>Sampsa Koponen from the Finnish Environment Institute (Syke) introduces the Vantaanjoki, a compact catchment with mixed land use that drains the Helsinki metropolitan region into the Gulf of Finland. You will see why Syke chose this long-term research catchment as a <a href="../../reference/glossary#land-sea-continuum">source-to-sea</a> use case for AquaINFRA, and which three questions the analysis sets out to answer.</p>

## Research questions

<div class="question-list">
    <ol>
        <li><strong>Organic carbon loads:</strong> How have the organic carbon loads that the river carries towards the sea varied over time?</li>
        <li><strong>Land use and hydrology:</strong> Can you identify relationships between changes in land use, river hydrology, and the material the river transports?</li>
        <li><strong>River signal at sea:</strong> Can the river signal be detected in coastal observations, and how does water quality change from the river mouth towards offshore waters?</li>
    </ol>
</div>

The first two questions use catchment and river data (Chapter 2). The third combines river discharge with ship and satellite observations of the coastal sea (Chapter 3).
