## What you can reuse

- **CORINE land cover tool**, in a European and a Finnish version, to quantify land cover change in any catchment.
- **River load workflow**, which turns concentration and discharge data into daily, monthly and annual loads for another river or another variable.
- **[Data-to-Knowledge Packages](../../reference/glossary#d2kp)** for teaching, bundling the data, workflow and results of the analysis.

You can find the published workflows and packages through the [AquaINFRA Interaction Platform](../../03_aip).
